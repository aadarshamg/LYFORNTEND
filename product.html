Formulary Matrix

The Olfactory Architecture

Formulated across 180 days of maceration. Each note emerges with disciplined cadence, oscillating from radiant citrus illumination to dense, resinous nocturne.

Head Register (0 - 30 Minutes)
Radiant & Piquant
Atlas Saffron Calabrian Bergamot Pink Peppercorn

An immediate luminous opening. Sun-ripened Italian citrus illuminates sharp, mineral saffron pistils from the high valleys of Taliouine, sparked by freshly cracked Madagascan pink berries.

Heart Register (30 Min - 4 Hours)
Velvet & Mystique
Grasse Rose de Mai Smoked Frankincense Night Jasmine

Centifolia roses hand-plucked at dawn are steeped in aromatic Hojari frankincense tears. A sultry nocturnal jasmine adds opulent creamy depth without compromising the smoked sacred silhouette.

Base Register (4 Hours - Indefinite)
Resinous & Earthbound
Smoked Ambergris Cambodian Wild Oud Sumatran Patchouli Golden Benzoin

An unforgettable magnetic foundation. Aged 14-year vintage agarwood oils unite with golden benzoin resins and earthy dark patchouli, anchoring the fragrance into an intoxicating, skin-warmed aura.

Harvest Origin

Atlas Valley Saffron Extract

31°03'N 7°54'W • Moroccan Atlas

It requires 75,000 hand-picked Crocus sativus flowers to yield a single pound of our signature saffron tincture. Aged in inert glass carboys for four lunations prior to blending.
