Unlike commercial formulations, every Leyros creation is distilled using the ancient Kannauj Deg-Bhapka hydro-distillation in copper vessels, then matured in French oak casks for eighteen lunar cycles before bottling. Our olfactory pyramid is engineered so that base accords linger intimately on linen and pulse points for up to 36 hours.
